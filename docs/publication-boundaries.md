# Publication boundaries for Lamat

Working decisions, 17 November 2026. The public repository and website must not expose private material merely because it informed an internal review.

| Material | Default decision | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing public articles and blog posts | Link in the dated historical archive | Preserve source, dates and known contributors; verify republication rights before copying full text or images. |
| Public historical notebooks and repositories | Link as historical work | Identify the original source and date; do not copy obsolete credentials, imply the old APIs work, or present old results as current. Check code licences before reuse. |
| New public research examples | Publish after ordinary review | Use synthetic or appropriately licensed data, document assumptions and provenance, and keep results reproducible. |
| Supplied Lamat logos and contact address | Use for the Lamat site | The owner provided these for this project. The image colour is sampled from pixels; original colour specifications and larger artwork can be added later. |
| Private projects, code, data, screenshots and names | Withhold pending specific owner approval | Keep private-source details and files out of public issues, commits, examples, screenshots and website copy; obtain a specific publication decision before publishing. |
| Unidentified collaborators, third-party content and data | Withhold or link only | Verify attribution, permission and licence before including content or data. Public accessibility does not itself grant republication rights. |
| Proposed current client work or consulting case studies | Withhold pending specific owner approval | Confirm the project, description, client consent and permitted evidence before adding it to the site. |

These are conservative working decisions, not a legal determination or an assertion that a private project is approved for disclosure. Do not add private source material to a public branch or pull request. Record any later approval at the level of the specific name, description, image, code or dataset being released.

## Decisions for currently proposed material

- **MeanReversion and Intelligence-Amplification:** link the public repositories as dated historical research; descriptions must distinguish the new synthetic examples from the historical work and its obsolete data services.
- **QTrader and Deep Q Trader:** link the owner-supplied public notebooks as historical teaching experiments; do not represent simulations as current products or evidence of trading performance.
- **Reddit, sentiment and LSTM gists:** link the public notebooks as historical experiments; verify data rights before reusing datasets or output.
- **Medium and Lamat articles:** link original publications with dates and available attribution; do not automatically republish full text or images.
- **Unannounced private or client case studies:** do not publish names, descriptions, screenshots, data or code unless the owner approves each specific item and any required third-party consent has been obtained.
