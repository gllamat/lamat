# Lamat website brief — working draft

Status: agreed direction, 17 November 2026. The owner supplied the logo, audience, contact address and navigation. Detailed design and individual project descriptions remain to be reviewed.

## Established direction

- Emphasize finance research and AI consulting for the site's audience.
- Use the supplied Lamat logo and draw visual direction from the current website.
- Use `contact@lamat-qanil.com` as the public contact address, as supplied by the owner.
- Present past work in a separate historical section, ordered by date and clearly identified as historical. Keep publication and revision dates distinct.
- Link to original publications and retain collaborators' attribution. Do not portray obsolete APIs or legacy research as current products.
- Keep private project content private unless its owner approves a specific public description.

## Brand and existing site

The owner supplied two 235 × 234 pixel logo images: [opaque](../apps/web/assets/lamat-logo.png) and [transparent](../apps/web/assets/lamat-logo-transparent.png). Both have a turquoise fill (`#009999`) and near-black outlines (`#050505`); the transparent version has transparent outer corners. These colours are measured from the provided image pixels, not an original brand-guide specification. Retain the originals and seek a higher-resolution or vector version if available for larger displays and print.

The existing home page has a white header, a bold dark LAMAT wordmark, a small grey tagline, simple Home / Blog / About / Contact navigation with a blue active link, a light grey diagram background, centered dark hero text and a white article section. It uses clean sans-serif type and generous spacing. Carry this restraint into the new design while incorporating the supplied turquoise logo. The existing prototype's `#3776b9` blue and text-only wordmark are provisional, not a verified brand specification.

The existing public [home page](https://lamatuk.com/) describes Lamat as data science applied to finance. Its [contact page](https://lamatuk.com/contact/) lists an older address and form. Use the newly supplied address for the new site; do not assume the old form is still monitored.

## Agreed navigation

Home; Work & Services; Approach; Historical Archive; About; Contact. Identify current projects and services when confirmed. Archive entries should include date, source, contributors when verified, and a link to the original.
