# Contact, subscriptions and site content decisions

For [issue #37](https://github.com/gllamat/lamat/issues/37), the owner confirmed on 26 November 2026:

- Use `contact@lamat-qanil.com` for public enquiries. The site provides email links; it does not have a contact form.
- Retire the legacy subscription service rather than replace it on the new site. No subscriber data is migrated, deleted, or contacted by this change. Disabling the existing WordPress/Jetpack service and deciding its stored-data retention require access to the relevant account and remain operational follow-up.
- Publish the previously protected historical demo. The recovered published fragment contains five original swap-spread chart links, a default 2–5 chart, DTCC attribution and six related article embeds. The replacement identifies the content as historical, links the original chart sources and articles, and omits the subscription placeholder. The separate draft and subscriber information are not part of this release. Historical chart data, current link availability and external service behavior may change; no historical output is represented as a current feed.
- The owner approved the proposed About/contact copy and brand assets.

## Verification and remaining operations

Check the contact links, demo chart and fallback links, layout and keyboard access on staging. A `mailto:` link can be inspected without sending a message, but this does not verify mailbox ownership, delivery or monitoring; a controlled end-to-end delivery check remains necessary. Verify retirement of the old subscription service in its own account and determine subscriber-data retention before deleting or transferring anything. Review historical data attribution, external dependencies and the route/redirect plan before cutover. Follow [site migration plan](site-migration-plan.md), [hosting options](site-hosting-options.md), and issues [#18](https://github.com/gllamat/lamat/issues/18), [#19](https://github.com/gllamat/lamat/issues/19) and [#37](https://github.com/gllamat/lamat/issues/37).
