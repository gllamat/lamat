2022-01-21

This historical article is reproduced from the original export. Some images and interactive elements are unavailable in this edition.

There are multiple publicly available data repositories compiling data from USD financial institutions, like DTCC (anonymized trade information with up to 15 minute lag) and SEC (full end of quarter asset disclosure with up to 3 months lag).
